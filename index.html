About me

My name is Malika Komilzhanova. I am a developer based in Almaty, Kazakhstan, building websites and tools with Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS and MySQL. I also create AI-generated video content. I enjoy turning ideas into clear, working products that look good and do their job.
